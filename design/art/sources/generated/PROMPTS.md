# Generated system artwork briefs

The four base images in this directory were created with an AI image-generation
tool during Frege's visual-design work. They are checked-in source artifacts,
not runtime generations.

The descriptions below record the creative concepts supplied to the tool. They
are not represented as verbatim prompts, model parameters, seeds, or recipes
that can regenerate the exact pixels. Generative-model behavior and tool
versions can change; the checked-in files are the source of record.

## Fragmentation

**File:** `fragmentation-base.webp`

An archival triptych of classical winged messengers isolated in separate
library bays. Each figure carries a different incomplete form of organizational
memory—torn map, broken tablet, or bundled documents—while a small evening star
remains visible above them. Deep Frege green, aged paper, engraved texture, and
no typography. The concept represents agent-local knowledge drifting into
incompatible copies.

## Context gate

**File:** `context-gate-base.webp`

A dark institutional archive feeding selected documents through an ornate
circular gate into a narrow beam of light. Most documents remain on the shelves;
only a scoped sequence passes the threshold. Deep green and warm paper tones,
fine engraved linework, generous negative space, and no typography. The concept
represents identity and policy resolving context before it reaches an agent.

## Provenance

**File:** `provenance-base.webp`

A vertical chain of disembodied archival hands passing a book, sealed envelope,
magnified source, revision note, and final bound volume along one unbroken
thread. Diamond registration marks connect the stages against a dark field.
Monochrome green engraving, paper highlights, and no typography. The concept
represents a decision that can be traced backward through its evidence.

## Persistence

**File:** `persistence-base.webp`

A classical observatory or institutional temple beneath a celestial system of
orbiting bodies and durable paths. The architecture remains fixed while models,
agents, and trajectories move around it. Night-sky black, Frege green, engraved
stars and clouds, symmetrical composition, and no typography. The concept
represents organizational memory persisting while agent runtimes change.
