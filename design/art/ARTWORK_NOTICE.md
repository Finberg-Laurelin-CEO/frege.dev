## Evelyn De Morgan — *Phosphorus and Hesperus*

Frege's architecture artwork is derived from Evelyn De Morgan's 1881 painting
*Phosphorus and Hesperus*. The deployable wide and mobile treatments preserve
the paired figures and torches while translating the scan into a deterministic
green, amber, and warm-paper ordered print.

The archived master is the original file published by Wikimedia Commons.
Commons marks the underlying painting and its faithful photographic
reproduction as public domain and free of known copyright restrictions, while
noting that PD-Art reuse rules can vary by jurisdiction. The De Morgan
Foundation collection page is cited as the authoritative object record; the
deployable file is sourced from Commons, not copied from the Foundation site.

The repository's proprietary software terms do not apply to the archived
public-domain master or to the generated De Morgan image assets in
`public/art/demorgan/`. Frege does not claim exclusive rights in the underlying
painting or source scan. Reusers remain responsible for confirming requirements
that may apply in their jurisdiction.

Full source metadata, source links, rights record, and checksum are recorded in
`design/art/sources/demorgan/SOURCE.md`. Deployable derivatives have their
metadata stripped and are regenerated by `scripts/art/render-demorgan.sh`.

## Generated Frege system artwork

The fragmentation, context, provenance, and persistence artwork in
`public/art/system/` begins with AI-generated base images created from
Frege-directed concepts. The concept briefs and tool-generated origin are
recorded in `design/art/sources/generated/PROMPTS.md`. The checked-in pixels,
not the prose briefs, are the source of record; exact AI regeneration is neither
claimed nor required by the build.

Those base images were processed locally in the browser with
[ASCII Magic](https://www.ascii-magic.com/), then archived as stripped source
masters in `design/art/sources/ascii-magic/`. ASCII Magic is credited as the
design-time processing tool. No ASCII Magic application code or example artwork
is redistributed in this repository, and this reference does not imply
affiliation or endorsement.

These system images are not part of the Hesperus public-domain carve-out. To the
extent copyright or related rights subsist in Frege's human-authored direction,
selection, arrangement, processing, and integration, those contributions remain
subject to the repository's proprietary notice. Frege does not claim rights that
applicable law does not recognize or rights retained by tool providers.

The browser-processing map is recorded in
`design/art/sources/ascii-magic/README.md`. Deployable encodings are regenerated
from the checked-in masters by `scripts/art/render-system-art.sh`.

## User-supplied photography

The corridor, sundial, and All Souls College images in `public/art/user/`
originate from photographs supplied directly by the repository owner. The
corridor and sundial were transformed locally with ASCII Magic and ImageMagick;
the All Souls panorama uses a deterministic ImageMagick ordered dither with an
HTML/CSS character layer on the docs page. The repository contains stripped,
downsampled masters and deployable encodings, not the original camera files or
their EXIF metadata.

These photographs are not part of the Hesperus public-domain carve-out and are
not represented as third-party stock imagery. Their processing record is in
`design/art/sources/user/README.md`; deployable encodings are regenerated by
`scripts/art/render-user-art.sh`.
